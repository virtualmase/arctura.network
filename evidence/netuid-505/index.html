Publication rule

The next evidence artifact must be verifiable on its own.

When a new testnet artifact is ready, this record should link to it rather than extend the narrative. Every added artifact should carry a UTC start and end time, the network and netuid, a reproducible method or command, an immutable URL or hash, the observed result, and its limitations.

  • Do not convert a local-testnet launch into a Finney claim.
  • Do not extend a bounded startup record to unrecorded concurrent scoring or weight results.
  • Do not present a simulated quorum meter as current network telemetry.
