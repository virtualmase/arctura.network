Arctura Network/Tools/Work Order

Prototype 001 · Local-first

Define the work before you train the agent.

This tool creates a clear work order for an agent. Nothing is uploaded or saved by Arctura. Your answers stay in this browser until you export or clear them.

Step 1 of 5
What useful work should be done?

Describe the result, not the technology.

What may the agent use?

Name the approved information and the things it must not use.

What authority does it have?

Give only the authority the result requires.

How will the result be checked?

Write checks that another person can apply.

What record should remain?

Keep enough evidence to inspect the work later.
