    06 · Respond

    Keep a rapid-response brief ready.

    07 · Preserve

    Export the complete campaign record.

    The open JSON record preserves the brief, methodology, audited sources, calculations, review state, press readiness, outreach context, and rapid-response plan.

    Boundary: “Launch ready” means the required preparation fields are complete. It does not mean the research is true, independently replicated, newsworthy, or likely to receive coverage.

    The eight gates

    No evidence, no headline.

    The record should make every editorial choice visible: what was counted, what was excluded, who checked it, and where the conclusion stops.

    01

    Story

    Testable hypothesis, timeliness, audience, and accountable owner.

    02

    Sample

    A declared target and cited records scored consistently.

    03

    Method

    Inclusion, exclusion, calculations, and limitations.

    04

    Review

    An independent human checks the method and findings.

    05

    Assets

    Dataset, methodology, charts, press facts, and spokesperson.

    06

    Finding

    A calculation generated from the records, not written first.

    07

    Relevance

    A named reporter, defined beat, and audience-specific reason.

    08

    Response

    Primary source, knowns, unknowns, control, and human approval.
