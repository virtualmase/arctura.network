What this isA practice environment

These guides turn the Work Standard into repeatable actions, artifacts, and review paths. They are educational methodology—not evidence that a specific agent capability is deployed.

How to use it

Enter where the work is weak. Continue until the record is reusable.

You do not need to complete every practice on every job. Choose the constraint in front of you, produce its proof artifact, and move forward only when the result can survive review.
