Scale a result, not an assumption

Repeat proven work with stable interfaces, explicit ownership, observable limits, and safe rollback.

Status: Published educational methodology. Use it as a review pattern, not as evidence that a specific agent or capability has been deployed.

Field practice

Run the practice

  1. 01

    Turn repeated inputs and outputs into a versioned contract.

  2. 02

    Keep identity, authority, and handoff points visible across every run.

  3. 03

    Add capacity only after reliability, cost, and failure behavior are measured.

Keep the proof

A versioned workflow with repeat-run results, operating limits, an owner, and a tested reversal path.

Connect the record to the precise conditions under which the work was performed. A clean result without its scope and limitations is not reusable evidence.
