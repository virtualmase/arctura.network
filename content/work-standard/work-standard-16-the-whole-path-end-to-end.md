# 16. The Whole Path, End to End

All fifteen parts connect into one line: a real question, checked work, an honest record, the right handoff, and a record that stays accurate after the fact. This is what "useful work" means at Arctura, spelled out in full.

## The path in order

1. **The real question is found** — [Part 11](/work-standard/finding-the-real-question/) — before anything else starts.
2. **Work happens and produces a change someone can check** — [Part 1](/work-standard/what-counts-as-work/).
3. **Any agent involved has its resources and authority mapped first** — [Part 4](/work-standard/before-an-agent-acts/), and if the work crosses into another system, its roles and handoffs are mapped too — [Part 7](/work-standard/working-with-other-systems/).
4. **Claims about the work separate fact from interpretation** — [Part 2](/work-standard/what-a-claim-needs/) — and every record involved has exactly one canonical version — [Part 3](/work-standard/one-record-per-thing/).
5. **If money or resources moved, that's tracked as its own separate state** — [Part 8](/work-standard/separating-what-moves/).
6. **Any decision made along the way passes the five checks** — [Part 9](/work-standard/how-decisions-get-bounded/) — and anything offered as a result of the work is stated precisely — [Part 10](/work-standard/what-gets-offered/).
7. **Evidence is assembled so the outcome can be checked independently** — [Part 12](/work-standard/what-counts-as-proof/) — and it's published in a form both people and machines can parse — [Part 6](/work-standard/readable-by-machines/).
8. **If an agent talks about any of this, it stays inside the same claim standard** — [Part 13](/work-standard/how-agents-talk/) — and hands off to a person cleanly wherever the decision requires one — [Part 14](/work-standard/handing-off-to-a-person/).
9. **The record doesn't stop being checked once it's published** — [Part 15](/work-standard/keeping-the-record-true/) — it's revisited, and corrected in the open if it turns out to be wrong.

## What this path is for

None of these fifteen parts is difficult on its own. What's hard is doing all of them, in order, without skipping the ones that feel unnecessary in the moment. This capstone exists as the checklist for that — not a new idea, just the whole standard held together in one place.

## The test for whether it worked

Ask, about any piece of finished work: could someone outside the network, with no context, follow this path backward from the result to the original question, checking every step against the record as they go? If yes, the standard held. If there's a gap anywhere in that chain, that's where to look first.
