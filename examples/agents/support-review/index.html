Worked example · Customer support

From “review this reply” to accountable agent work.

This example shows how a narrow support-review agent can be described, bounded, evaluated, and handed back to a person without granting it authority to contact a customer.

Evidence statusIllustrative example

Useful as a template. Not a production claim or independent test.

Boundary: The organization and evaluation below are fictional. The records demonstrate the Arctura method; they do not establish that a deployed support agent passed this test.

The operating boundary

The agent reviews. A person decides and sends.

Purpose

Catch unsupported claims

Review one approved draft against supplied policy excerpts and return flags for human review.

Responsible owner

Support Operations

The team owns the prompt, approved policies, access, evaluation, and final customer decision.

Allowed

Read, compare, flag

  • Read the supplied draft.
  • Compare it with supplied policy.
  • Flag conflicts and missing support.
Prohibited

No external action

  • No customer messages.
  • No account access.
  • No refunds or binding decisions.
