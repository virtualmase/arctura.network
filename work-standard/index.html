Arctura Network/The Work Standard

Open methodology · Version 0.1

The standard behind useful work.

Sixteen connected principles for turning a real question into bounded work, inspectable evidence, a responsible handoff, and a record that stays true.

Explore all 16 partsPut it into practice →
Current statusPublished methodology

The standard describes how Arctura intends work to be framed and reviewed. Individual capabilities are only operational where a linked implementation or evidence record says they are.

Figure 01 The complete Work Standard, arranged as one connected accountability path.

Why it exists

A capable system can still do the wrong work.

Quality is not only a model question. It depends on whether the job was understood, authority was bounded, checks were chosen in advance, claims match the record, and a person remains accountable where judgment is required.

Review framework

Five checks before change.

This is a decision lens, not evidence of a DAO, formal council membership, or on-chain vote.

  1. 01Need

    Is there a real reason?

  2. 02Clarity

    Is the change understood?

  3. 03Usefulness

    Does it solve the named problem?

  4. 04Durability

    Will the decision hold up?

  5. 05Reversal

    Can the system change course?
