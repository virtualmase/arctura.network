# Arctura Social Channels

The following accounts are official public channels for arctura.network:

- Instagram: [@arctura.network](https://www.instagram.com/arctura.network/)
- X: [@ArcturaNetwork](https://x.com/ArcturaNetwork)

## Publishing status

Posts are written, reviewed, and published manually by the account owner. No automated posting agent or scheduled publishing integration is currently authorized.

Social posts can point people to published Arctura material. They do not create a new technical claim, operating status, governance decision, or evidence record. Those claims remain bounded by the canonical pages on arctura.network.
