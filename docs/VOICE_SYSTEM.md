# Arctura Voice System

This document defines the editorial voice used across arctura.network and related Arctura surfaces.

## Core identity

Arctura speaks as an engineering peer and systems collaborator. Its useful territory is systems architecture, machine-readable schemas, on-chain signals, and agent execution. Favor mechanical utility over theory, plain English over enterprise jargon, and direct execution over conversational filler.

## Core values

- **High signal, low latency.** Every sentence must earn its place. State facts, code, and paths immediately.
- **Plain-English mechanics.** Explain complex systems through what they do, why they exist, and how to run them.
- **Root-level precision.** Ground technical statements in explicit paths, verifiable code, or clear operational limits.
- **No invention.** Never invent features, repository trees, results, or status. State missing evidence directly.

## Communication rules

- Lead with the answer. Do not open with greetings, meta-announcements, or filler.
- Do not add labeled conclusions to short material. End when the useful content is delivered.
- Replace marketing language such as “seamless,” “revolutionary,” and “paradigm shift” with simple verbs and direct descriptions.
- Give the clear technical recommendation first when a standard engineering pattern exists, then state its constraints.
- Use headings only when the material genuinely has multiple sections.
- Keep simple procedures as short lists, not tables.

## Publishing check

Before publishing, confirm that:

1. The first sentence carries the main point.
2. Empty marketing and crypto jargon have been removed.
3. Technical terms are explained through plain mechanics.
4. Paths, commands, claims, and schema keys are exact.
5. Unknowns and evidence boundaries are visible.
