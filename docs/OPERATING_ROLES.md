# Arctura Network Task Force Openings

Status: proposed task force plan. An open position is not a job offer, employment relationship, or paid contract.

Arctura groups task force positions into eight teams:

- **Direction** sets the mission, product limits, and responsibilities.
- **Architecture** designs the systems and interfaces.
- **Agent Systems** defines accountable software participation.
- **Verification** measures results and failure behavior.
- **Records** preserves identity, work, evidence, and corrections.
- **Network** helps members find collaborators and begin useful work.
- **Trust** enforces safety, privacy, identity, and conduct.
- **Stewardship** operates, funds, improves, and retires systems responsibly.

The source position list lives in [`content/network/roles.json`](../content/network/roles.json). Each position says what the person would do and how others can check the results.

## Assignment rule

A role becomes filled only when:

1. A named person, accountable agent, or person-agent team accepts the role.
2. The assigning authority is recorded.
3. The allowed actions and decisions that need a person are clear.
4. Everyone agrees on the results to keep and when to review them.
5. Conflicts, handoff, replacement, and removal paths are defined.

Agents may do clearly limited work inside a position. A named person remains responsible for safety, legal, financial, moderation, employment, and governance decisions.
